Privacy policy

What we keep,
and what we don’t.

ProveIt needs a few things to hold you to a habit: an account, your proof photos, and the names of the friends you bring in. Most of what it reads, like Apple Health and your location, never leaves your phone. This page says exactly what goes where.

03What stays on your phone

These are read on your iPhone and are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Apple Health. If you connect it, ProveIt reads the workouts, runs, sleep and weigh-ins your commitments need, so a session can count on its own. The readings stay on the phone. What leaves is only that a session was counted.
  • Location. If you pick a place for a habit, ProveIt checks your phone is there when you tap “I’m here” or take a photo. Only while you’re using the app, and where your phone is never leaves it. The place you picked is part of your commitment, so it’s kept in your backup (see below).
  • Contacts. If you choose a friend from your contacts, ProveIt reads that one contact to fill in their name. Nothing else in your address book is read.
  • Alarms and reminders. ProveIt’s wake-up alarm and its reminders are scheduled on the phone.

Each of these asks for iOS permission first, and you can turn it off at any time in the iPhone’s Settings.

04What we keep on our server, and why

When you sign in, ProveIt keeps the following on its server so your commitments survive a new phone and your friends can hold you to them:

Your account

  • The account ID Sign in with Apple gives us, and the name and email Apple shares. You can choose to hide your email: Apple then gives us a private relay address instead.
  • Your display name, username and profile photo if you add them, your time zone, and the app language, so reminders arrive at the right time in the right language.

Your commitments

  • A backup of what you set up and how it went: habits, targets, stakes (practice money), settled weeks, any place you picked for a habit (its name, address and spot on the map), and any weigh-ins you type in yourself.
  • Your proofs: the photo, when it was taken, which habit it was for, how it was proved (camera, tap or Apple Health), and the verdicts and any appeal. For a proof at the place you picked, the verdict says how far from it your phone was.

Your friends and groups

  • The names you give people you invite, their invite codes, the groups and challenges you make or join, and the messages in those threads. Messages are preset lines (nudges and cheers), plus any proof photos you send.

Running the service

  • Your device’s push token, so notifications can reach your phone, and the device type (“iPhone”) saved with your backup.
  • Short-lived counters of how often an account does certain things, like sending nudges, so nobody can spam or abuse the service.
  • Our hosting provider’s request logs, which include your IP address. They’re used to keep the service secure and to fix problems, and they’re deleted automatically within days.

Why we’re allowed to. We use this data to provide the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and keep abuse counters and request logs because we have a legitimate interest in keeping ProveIt safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f)). A weigh-in is health information, so we keep it, and send it for the photo check, only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)). You give that consent by entering it, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

05How photo checks work

When you submit a proof photo, our server sends it to Anthropic’s Claude API with what it needs to judge it: the habit’s name, what counts as proof, your target for a session, the local time the photo was taken and, for a weigh-in, the weight you typed. Claude tells us whether the photo looks like what the habit asks for. Anthropic processes it only to return that answer. Under its commercial terms, it doesn’t use the photo to train its models.

The answer is advice, not a ruling. ProveIt never rejects a photo on its own. If the check isn’t sure, the photo goes to your referee to decide, or stands after 24 hours.

Proofs proved by a tap or by Apple Health are not sent to Anthropic.

06What your friends can see

When you connect with someone, as a referee, partner, group or challenge, they can see:

  • Your name and profile photo.
  • One line about your week: the habit name and how many sessions you’ve done out of how many.
  • The proof photos of any commitment you point at them, so they can make the call on one. For a weigh-in commitment, that’s the photo of your scale, number included.
  • For a proof at the place you picked, the place’s name and how far from it your phone was.
  • In a challenge, its terms: the stake and where the money goes. Everyone in it agrees to the same ones.
  • The nudges, cheers and photos in the threads you share.

Outside a challenge, they never see what you staked. They never see your balance, your Apple Health numbers or where your phone is.

08What we never do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • No payment details. During the beta all money in ProveIt is practice money, and there’s no card to take.
  • No camera-roll access. ProveIt only uses photos you take in the app.

09How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account.

Settings → Delete account deletes your account at once: every commitment, proof photo and week, on your phone and on our server, and everything your friends could see of you. It can’t be undone. Friends who had invited you keep the name they typed for you, with nothing of yours behind it.

Settings → Sign out is different: your phone forgets everything, and your account keeps it for when you sign back in.

A deleted account can survive in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short time, until those backups are replaced. Abuse counters keep only a running count per action, the record of which notifications were sent is deleted after 30 days, and request logs are deleted within days.

10Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and send you a copy.
  • Correct something that’s wrong.
  • Delete it. The app does this immediately, or we can on request.
  • Give it to you in a portable format.
  • Stop or limit a use of it, or withdraw a consent you gave.

Email rahalhabib2006@gmail.com and we’ll answer within one month. If you’re in the EU and unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Spain, that’s the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

11Security

Everything between the app and our server is encrypted in transit. The database is locked so the app can only use a fixed set of checked actions: no one can read tables directly, even with the app’s key. Proof photos are stored privately and are only handed out to you and the people you share them with. No system is perfectly secure, but we test ours against attacks before every change.

12Age

ProveIt is for people 18 and over, because it’s built around staking money. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, tell us and we’ll delete it.

13Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect or who sees it, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies.
