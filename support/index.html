01The beta

How do I join the beta?

Install TestFlight from the App Store. It’s Apple’s app for trying apps before they launch. Then open the invite link you were sent, and tap Install.

No link yet? Ask for one.

Is any real money involved?

No. During the beta every stake is practice money. Nothing is charged, there’s no card to add, and nothing is paid out or donated. It all works the same way, so you can feel what a stake does.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Take a screenshot while you’re in ProveIt, then tap Share Beta Feedback. Or open TestFlight, choose ProveIt and tap Send Beta Feedback. Either way it comes straight to us, with your iPhone model and iOS version attached.

If the app crashes, TestFlight asks if you want to say what happened. Please do.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 17 or later. The wake-up alarm needs iOS 26. On earlier versions, you prove a wake-up with a tap or a photo instead.

02Proof

How does ProveIt check my photo?

A few rules run on your phone first. A repeat, a photo too soon after the last one, one taken too late, or one taken far from the place you picked gets flagged. Then ProveIt’s checker looks at the photo and says whether it shows what the habit asks for.

It never rejects a photo on its own. If it isn’t sure, the photo goes to your referee to decide. If you don’t have one, it stands after 24 hours.

Why can’t I use a photo from my camera roll?

Because an old photo isn’t proof you went today. Proof photos are taken in the app, at the time, or they don’t count.

A photo was judged wrong. What now?

Open the proof and appeal it. Your referee sees the photo and your reason, and makes the call. A referee who doesn’t answer within 24 hours counts as a yes.

Can a habit prove itself?

Yes. Connect Apple Health in Settings → Connections, and workouts, runs and sleep that reach your target count on their own. That includes ones from an Apple Watch, or from apps like Strava and Whoop that write to Health. The numbers stay on your phone.

You can also pick your gym on a map, then tap “I’m here” when you arrive. Wake-ups and custom habits can be proved with an “I’m up” or “I did it” tap.

03Friends and referees

How do I add a friend as my referee?

In Groups, tap Add someone and send them the invite. Then open a commitment and choose them under Referee. For a group, share the group link instead. Everyone in it can make the call on a photo you point at the group.

What can my friends see?

Your name, how many sessions you’ve done this week, and the proof photos of commitments you point at them. In a challenge, they also see the stake you all agreed to. Otherwise they never see what you staked, and never your balance, your Apple Health numbers or where your phone is. More in the privacy policy.

How do nudges work?

A nudge is a one-tap line, like “Still time.”, sent to someone who hasn’t done it yet. You can nudge each person once a day. They’re set lines, so there’s nothing to moderate and nothing to regret.

04Settings

How do I change when ProveIt reminds me?

Settings → Daily nudge sets the time. ProveIt only nudges you on days nothing’s been proved yet. To turn notifications off completely, use the iPhone’s Settings → Notifications → ProveIt.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language: English, Español or Français. It applies the next time you open the app.

Where is Settings?

The gear at the top right of Today or Profile.

05Your account

How do I get my commitments onto a new phone?

Install ProveIt and choose Already on ProveIt? Sign in on the first screen, with the same Apple ID. Everything comes back.

What’s the difference between signing out and deleting?

Sign out makes this phone forget everything, while your account keeps it for when you sign back in. Delete account removes every commitment, photo and week, on the phone and on our server, at once. It can’t be undone.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It happens immediately. If you can’t open the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we’ll do it for you.
