How it works

Three steps.
No way around them.

ProveIt doesn’t cheer you on or send you badges. It holds what you said you’d do, checks that you did it, and settles up when the week ends.

01Choose

Say it.

Pick what you want to be held to, and how often. A target that means something: minutes, kilometres, the time you’re up.

  • Gym3× a week
  • Read20 min · daily
  • Wake up early06:30 · 5 mornings
  • Run5 km · 2× a week
02Commit

Stake it.

Put money on the week. Hit it and you keep it. Miss it and it goes to a cause you pick. Pick one you’d rather not fund if you want the extra push.

  • StakeYou set it, per week
  • Where it goesA cause, never to us
  • SettlesWhen the week ends
  • During the betaPractice money
03Prove

Do it.

Prove each session as it happens. A photo from the camera, an “I’m up” tap at your wake-up time, or a workout that Apple Health already logged. The camera only: an old photo isn’t proof you went today.

ProveIt reads the photo. It never rejects one on its own: a photo that looks off goes to your referee, or stands after 24 hours.

Friends

Bring a referee.

Add a friend or start a group. They see whether you showed up, never what you staked. If a photo looks off, they make the call. No vote within 24 hours counts as a yes.

Nudges are one tap and only go to whoever hasn’t done it yet. They’re set lines, so nobody types anything they’ll regret.

The rules ProveIt keeps

Strict with you.
Straight with you.

An app that takes your money when you slip had better be honest about everything else.

Camera only

Proof is taken, not found.

No camera roll. The photo is taken in the app, at the time, or it doesn’t count.

Never auto-rejects

A person makes the call.

When a photo looks wrong, your referee decides. You get one appeal, and silence counts for you.

Health stays put

Your numbers don’t leave.

Workouts, runs and sleep from Apple Health are read on your phone. Friends see “counted”, never the numbers.

No ads, no tracking

You’re not the product.

No ad networks, no analytics SDKs, nothing sold. Read the privacy policy.

Your record

Every week,
on the record.

Weeks cleared, proofs taken, money kept and money given. Every photo is filed by month. At the end of each month, and in December for the whole year, a short wrap arrives on its own.
